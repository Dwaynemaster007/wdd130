Our River Excursions

We provide a variety of river operations tailored to your target difficulty level and timeline. All packages include standard personal flotation gear, helmets, specialized watercraft, and an expert guide.

Have custom group sizing requirements or special questions? Contact Our Booking Office

Available Packages

The Half-Day Splash

Perfect for beginners, corporate outings, and families with young children. This excursion features mild Class II rapids interspersed with spectacular, scenic canyon views and swimming spots.

The Salmon Wilderness Run

Our most popular full-day experience. Traverse thrilling Class III to IV rapids winding through rugged, deep volcanic gorges. A gourmet riverside deli lunch is included midway through your journey.

The Class IV Thunder Gorge

An uncompromising, highly technical challenge built exclusively for seasoned thrill-seekers. Prepare for non-stop action, rapid vertical drops, and highly rigorous paddling conditions under expert guidance.
